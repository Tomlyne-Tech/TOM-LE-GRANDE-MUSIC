Who is Tom Le Grande

Called to create.
Anointed to lead.

The name "Le Grande" is drawn from the French Le Grand the great, the noble. Not personal greatness, but the greatness of a calling: excellence in service, leadership through worship, and creativity shaped entirely by purpose.

At the keysStudio Session

Tom Le Grande is a Music Director, Pianist, and Worship Creative whose life is built on faith, excellence, leadership, and purpose. Every sound is treated as a sacred assignment — every gift, a workmanship prepared in advance.

From the rehearsal room to the platform, Tom's approach fuses musical precision with spiritual conviction: charts marked with the same care as prayers whispered before a set, a choir tuned the same way a heart is postured toward God.

The testimony is simple every instrument, every arrangement, every room he leads is an act of worship first, and a performance second. Purpose precedes production.

  • I.

    Faith

    The unseen foundation beneath every arrangement and every note.

  • II.

    Worship

    Music as an offering sound surrendered before it is performed.

  • III.

    Excellence

    Craft honoured as an act of honour nothing brought half-prepared.

  • IV.

  • V.

    Every creation carries an assignment prepared long before it is played.

"I believe that every sound, every gift, and every creation carries a divine purpose. Through music, worship, and excellence, I seek to inspire, lead, and glorify God."

The Role

What a Music Director carries

Beyond the platform, direction is the quiet architecture behind every worship moment built long before the first chord sounds.

Worship Leadership

Setting the spiritual and musical direction of a service discerning flow, key, and moment.

Choir & Band Direction

Uniting voices and instruments into one coherent, Spirit-led sound.

Arrangement & Composition

Shaping charts and progressions that serve the song's heart, not just its structure.

Rehearsal Direction

Turning a room of individual musicians into a single, unified instrument.

Sound Design & Production

Crafting sonic texture in the room and in the recording booth alike.

Team Mentorship

Raising up musicians and worshippers leadership as discipleship.
